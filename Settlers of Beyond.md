Settlers of Beyond: A survival strategy/post-apocalyptic shooter/MMORPG game that draws strong cues from Settlers of Catan. It will be a mobile-style game (i.e. touch friendly), but will have a web client as well.

The Basics

1. Heros  
2. Resources  
   1. Food  
   2. Water: Dirty for tasks & Clean for drinking  
   3. Structural: Straw / Plastic / Wood / Metal  
   4. Cloth: Wool / Cotton  
   5. Electricity  
3. Weapons  
   1. Melee (sticks/clubs/pipes)  
   2. Silent projectiles (bow & arrow, cross-bow)  
   3. Power projectiles (hand-guns, SMGs, heavy machine guns, rocket launchers)  
   4. Static (traps/explosives)  
4. NPCs  
   1. Animals  
   2. Zombies  
   3. Robots  
   4. Shops  
5. Troops  
6. Technology  
   1. Vehicles  
   2. Structures

Heros have 5 levels of power/value: common (green), uncommon (blue), rare (purple), ultra-rare (gold), and maximum (diamond). They are the main representation of the player's power. They are represented by playing cards, and have different stats associated with them: Attack, Defense, Intelligence, which combine to make Power.

The primary method of advancing in the game (at the beginning) is by completing challenges. Challenges will involve some interaction between the player and NPCs. Examples: hunting animals to gain wool/food, crafting clothing with wool, clearing zombies to gain land, gathering resources by chopping down trees, scavenging technology by solving puzzles.

There are 3 main views of the game (outside of challenges): HQ, World, Beyond.

HQ consists of square sections representing different player structures:

1. Headquarters  
2. Farms to gather food/wool  
3. Lumber mills to gather wood  
4. Water pump to make water  
5. Mechanic makes/fixes vehicles  
6. Gunsmith makes bullets  
7. Training camps to improve troop experience  
8. Warehouses protect stored resources  
9. Hospitals to heal wounded troops

World is represented by a large geographically-diverse land, shown isometrically by hexagonal tiles that contain other peoples’ HQs and higher-level NPCs (i.e. bosses) / specialty shops / hotspots (areas that have significant amounts of resources).

“Beyond” zooms back to a galactic level. As the game grows, the number of players in a given world should be limited/balanced, so there will be multiple worlds. Space travel will be an option much later in the game.

Once the player completes enough challenges to gather enough resources

The player starts off with one common Hero: Jack. Jack has a stick and a hand-gun with 20 bullets.

There are also alliances that the player will want to join after getting through phase 1\. 

Once the player completes the tutorial, they are able to go outside of their HQ and see the “world”.

The world is represented isometrically by hexagonal tiles that contain other peoples’ HQs and higher-level NPCs.

The game will eventually be playable on mobile, but I'd like to start with a web version first.
