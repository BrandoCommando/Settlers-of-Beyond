Settlers of Beyond: A survival strategy/post-apocalyptic shooter/MMORPG game that draws strong cues from Settlers of Catan. It will be a mobile-style game (i.e. touch friendly), but will have a web client as well.

# The Basics

1. Heros  
2. Resources  
   1. Food  
   2. Water: Dirty for tasks & Clean for drinking  
   3. Structural: Straw, Plastic, Wood, Metal  
   4. Cloth: Wool, Cotton, Leather, Kevlar
   5. Electricity
   6. Currency: Coins, Rubies, Diamonds, Gold Bars
3. Weapons  
   1. Melee (sticks/clubs/pipes/ax)
   2. Silent projectiles (bow & arrow, cross-bow) are stealthy
   3. Power projectiles (chainsaw, hand-guns, SMGs, heavy machine guns, rocket launchers) are much louder, and may warrant unwanted attention.
   4. Static (traps/explosives)
4. NPCs
   1. Animals
   2. Zombies
   3. Robots
   4. Shops
5. Troops
6. Technology
   1. Vehicles
   2. Structures

Heros have 5 levels of power/value: common (green), uncommon (blue), rare (purple), ultra-rare (gold), and maximum (diamond). They are the main representation of the player's power. They are represented by playing cards, and have different stats associated with them: Attack, Defense, Intelligence, which combine to make Power.

# User interface
There are 3 main views of the game (outside of challenges): HQ, World, Beyond.

## HQ
HQ consists of square sections representing different player structures:

1. Headquarters  
2. Farms to gather food/wool  
3. Lumber mills to gather wood  
4. Water pump to make water  
5. Mechanic makes/fixes vehicles
6. Gunsmith makes bullets
7. Houses for troops that also gain EXP
8. Training camps to improve troop experience
9. Warehouses protect stored resources
10. Hospitals to heal wounded troops

## World
World is represented by a large geographically-diverse land, shown isometrically by hexagonal tiles:
1. Players' HQs take one hexagon
2. Higher-level NPCs (i.e. bosses) can take one or more hexagons
3. Specialty shops
4. Hotspots are areas that have significant amounts of resources/technology

## Beyond
“Beyond” zooms back to a galactic level. As the game grows, the number of players in a given world should be limited/balanced, so there will be multiple worlds. Space travel will be an option much later in the game.

# Gameplay

## Challenges
The primary method of advancing in the game (at the beginning at least) is by completing challenges. Challenges will involve some interaction between the player and NPCs.

Examples:
1. Hunting animals to gain cloth/food
2. Crafting clothing with raw cloth
3. Clearing zombies to gain land
4. Gathering resources by chopping down trees
5. Scavenging technology by solving puzzles

## Story
The player starts off with one common Hero: Jack. Jack has an axe and a hand-gun with 20 bullets. He needs to find a way to survive.

Once the player completes the tutorial, they are able to go outside of their HQ and see the “world”. There they will be exposed to alliances.

## Aliances
Aliances are the main social aspect of the game. Once the player joins an alliance, they will be able to communicate with them, and will benefit from shared stats. Aliances can claim territories (larger defined groups of spaces).
